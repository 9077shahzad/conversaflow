Workbook: `ConversaFlow-Pricing-Support-Model.xlsx`. It has 244 formulas, recalculated with no errors, and every
assumption can be edited.

- **Approved:** the separate 10-seat allowance per pilot company.
- **Proposals:** the 90-day pilot length, the pilot spending caps, the support offer and the annual prices (10 × monthly).

Nothing is published or offered.

## What each plan's support includes, and what it excludes

This is the basis for every margin figure below.

| | Starter ($29) | Growth ($79) | Business ($199) | Enterprise (from $499) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Included support time each month | 15 minutes | 45 minutes | 3 hours (scenario B: 2 hours) | 6 hours |
| Channels | Email | Email and chat | Email, chat, call by appointment | Email, chat, calls, named contact |
| First response (business hours, Sun–Thu, Gulf time) | Next business day | Same business day | Within 4 business hours | Within 2 business hours |
| What included time covers | How-to questions, help with settings, checking a fault | As Starter, plus team set-up and template wording | As Growth, plus a monthly 30-minute review | As Business, plus a quarterly review; contract terms |
| Faults in ConversaFlow itself | Always free, never counted | Always free | Always free | Always free |
| Unused time | Does not carry over | Does not carry over | Does not carry over | By contract |
| Onboarding | Self-service; assisted $99, optional (about 1.5 h) | $199 one-off (about 3 h) | $399 one-off (about 6 h) | From $799 (about 12 h) |
| Extra support | $60 an hour, in 30-minute steps, agreed before work starts | Same | Same | In contract or $60 an hour |

**Not included in any plan** (Enterprise only where its contract says so):

- building custom integrations or automations;
- writing or running campaigns;
- Meta account, billing or policy disputes, which are handled with Meta;
- training beyond onboarding;
- data migration from other tools, which is quoted separately;
- on-site visits;
- out-of-hours support.

**How the margin counts support:**

- The cost is the included time actually used, at the hourly rate.
- Extra time is sold at $60 an hour and costed at the hourly rate.
- The onboarding fee and onboarding time are both spread over an assumed 24-month customer life.
- Customers are assumed to ask for 30 minutes, 1.5, 3 and 6 hours a month, by plan.
- 40% of the time above the allowance is assumed to be bought as extra support.

## Pilot subsidy (proposals: 90 days, caps)

The proposed terms are no ConversaFlow fee, with each pilot company paying Meta directly.

| **Expected subsidy** | **$5,224** | **$7,837** |
| **Proposed cap** (plus 15%, rounded up to the next $500) | **$6,500** | **$9,500** |

The subsidy excludes the independent security review, legal drafting and development time. Spending would be
tracked monthly, with a review with you at 80% of the cap.
